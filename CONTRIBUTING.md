# Contributing

Open an issue before changing a normative field or verification claim.

Every schema change must include:

- a valid fixture;
- a failing fixture for each new rejection rule;
- compatibility notes; and
- an update to the normative documentation.

Draft releases may change incompatibly. Candidate and stable releases follow
semantic versioning: additive optional fields are minor changes; changed
meaning, required fields, canonicalization, or digest rules are major changes.

Do not submit private source, credentials, provider URLs, internal host or
database identifiers, customer data, or unredacted build logs.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under
Apache-2.0.
